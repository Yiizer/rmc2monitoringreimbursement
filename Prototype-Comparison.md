The original homepage continues to show the current design. The comparison snapshots can remain unchanged while future work continues on the main prototype.

Both versions contain fictional data and simulated email actions. Access remains private to the existing site owner; these links do not grant other people access.

For a fair comparison, ask each person to open a request, check its documents, and prepare a sample email in both versions. Ask which version made the next step clearer and where they hesitated.

Offline backups: `RMC2-version-a.zip` and `RMC2-version-b.zip`. Extract each into its own folder and open `index.html`.
